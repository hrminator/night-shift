What this is. After the reassess turn has written its decision record for a night in which the revised quote Q-1187-B (the scan-styled, image-only PDF) was read, the app makes one separate grounding call: it sends the rendered page as a PNG plus "locate this line", using the exact line text Spark quoted in its claim about the revised quote. The call runs outside the model's decision context, behind a flag, and failure is tolerated: a failed, empty or off-target result logs `line_not_located` and changes nothing else. This call does not detect the change; Spark found the change in its own reading of the page during the reassess turn. B1 only points at the line Spark quoted. Say exactly that in BUILDLOG.md and in the docstring.

Facts copied from Meta's docs (object grounding / vision pages for the Responses API):
  - Model `muse-spark-1.3` on `POST https://api.meta.ai/v1/responses`.
  - Content is an `input_image` with a data URL (`data:image/png;base64,...`) plus `input_text` in one user message. Send our own PNG, not the PDF: the file endpoint returns no coordinates for page images. PNG at most 50 MB inline.
  - Keep the system prompt minimal and put the grounding instruction in the user message. Use the doc's wording: "You are an object grounding expert. Provide the bounding box coordinates of ...", "The coordinates should be in the 0-1000 range" and "If you cannot find an object, omit it."
  - Output goes through `text.format` with `type: "json_schema"`, not `response_format`. Schema: {"object_name": string, "bbox": [{"x_min","y_min","x_max","y_max"}]}, no recursion, additionalProperties false.
  - Coordinates are on a 0-1000 grid with (0,0) at the top left. Clamp to 0-1000, because 1001 occurs. In CSS, a value divided by 10 is the percentage.
  - Medium effort needs `max_output_tokens` of at least 2048. Use low effort first; if the probe fails at low, try medium once.
  - Send `prompt_cache_key` for call type `ground` like the other call types (F3).
